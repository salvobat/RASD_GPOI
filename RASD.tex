\section{Introduction}
\subsection{Purpose}
The purpose of StudentHub is to provide a platform designed by students, for students. Its objective is to streamline the management of student life. The platform will allow students to manage their tasks and organise their study sessions. The platform will also provide a space for students to connect with each other, share resources and collaborate on projects. The platform will also offer students a network forum where they can post announcements to find used books and private lessons, as well as share information about school events.

Elir, a first-year Computer Science student, creates an account on StudentHub using his university email. He then creates a "Biology Midterm Prep" study group, making it discoverable to other Biology students. Alessio invites five classmates, three of whom accept immediately. \textbf{Domain Model Description:}\\
This domain model represents the core structure of StudentHub, a platform designed to streamline student life management. The User entity is at the core of the system, with connections to all the major components. The Calendar interface allows users to create and manage deadlines, offering options for both private and shared academic tasks. The Note entity facilitates the uploading and sharing of study materials, organised by subject and tags.
\\
StudyGroups facilitate collaboration between users, enabling members to exchange messages and coordinate study sessions. The creation of a group involves the sending of invitations to potential members. The Forum contains an Announcements section where users can post requests for books, offer tutoring services, or share event information. Should users wish to interact with announcements, they are able to do so through the 'Comments' function.
\\
The model also includes a notification system that keeps users informed about deadlines, group invitations and forum activity. The relationships between entities reflect the platform's collaborative nature, demonstrating how users interact with content and each other.
\\
This structure supports all of the identified goals of the RASD, including account management, deadline tracking, resource sharing, group collaboration, forum interactions, and marketplace functionality, creating a comprehensive ecosystem for student academic life management.

\subsection{Product functions}
This section describes the main functions of the system, focusing on the actions that users can perform and the interactions between them and the system.
\subsubsection{Student Functions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Create an account}: a student provides the system his/her full name and valid institutional email, the student chooses a password and confirms it. The system sends a confirmation of entry email to validate. When confirmed, the account is created.
    \item \textbf{Delete an account}: the student enters his/her profile settings and clicks on the delete account button, the system displays a label where the student has to confirm the password. If the password is correct the account is deleted.
    \item \textbf{Log in}: the student enters his email and password. If the data are correct, he/she is logged in and accesses the global forum; otherwise, an error is displayed.
    \item \textbf{Log out}: The student clicks on the logout button on the screen, the system asks for confirmation, and if "yes" is clicked, the student is logged out.
    \item \textbf{Create a deadline}: the student clicks on the add deadline button on the calendar page, and the system displays a modal for the deadline name and description. The student enters the requested data and clicks "Publish".
    \item \textbf{Move a deadline}: the student clicks and drags with the mouse the deadline he/she wants to move and drops it on the proper date.
    \item \textbf{Publish some notes/an announcement}: the student on the proper page clicks on the new button and inserts his data, the student uploads eventual attachments. The student then clicks on publish.
    \item \textbf{Edit a deadline/notes/an announcement}:  the student selects the item he/she wants to edit and clicks on the edit button. The student edits what he/she needs and confirms the new data by clicking on "Publish".
    \item \textbf{Delete a deadline/notes/an announcement}: the student selects the item he/she wants to delete and clicks on the delete button. A confirmation modal appears; the student clicks on confirm.
    \item \textbf{Send chat message on a study group}: The student goes to the study group page, types the message in the message box and then clicks send.
    \item \textbf{Add a comment to an announcement}: The student enters the comment in the comment box below the announcement and then clicks send.
    \item \textbf{Get the share link of an announcement}: The student clicks on the share icon on the announcement he/she wants to share and the link is copied.
    \item \textbf{Search a topic on the forum}: The student enters the topic in the search bar and clicks the search icon. The list of documents related to the searched topic will be displayed.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{User Characteristics}
This section describes the general characteristics of the intended groups of users.
\subsubsection{Student characteristics}
The students who will use this platform are usually enrolled at a university, so they are most likely to be between 18 and 30 years old and certainly have basic digital skills. A device with a stable connection is required to access the platform, as it all works by communicating with others. The platform provides translation in the most common languages, so users are only expected to have basic reading skills in one of the supported languages and do not require advanced technical knowledge beyond standard web and app usage. The platform should use a user interface with a high-contrast palette to improve the experience of visually impaired users and be compatible with screen readers (for example, using alternative text for images), ensuring accessibility for users with various disabilities. In general, it should be accessible to all types of users. Students are expected to access the platform regularly, either daily or weekly, to manage deadlines, share resources, and interact with study groups.

\section{Specific Requirements}

\subsection{External Interface Requirements}

\subsubsection{User Interfaces}
The StudentHub platform will provide the following user interfaces:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Landing Page}: Displays general information about the platform and provides access to login and registration.
    \item \textbf{Dashboard}: Displays a summary of the user's calendar, deadlines, recent notes, and notifications.
    \item \textbf{Calendar Interface}: Shows shared and private deadlines in a calendar view with daily, weekly, and monthly views.
    \item \textbf{Study Notes Repository}: Interface for uploading, searching, and managing study notes.
    \item \textbf{Study Group Interface}: For creating, managing, and participating in study groups.
    \item \textbf{Forum Interface}: For creating, viewing, and interacting with announcements.
    \item \textbf{Marketplace Interface}: For listing and searching books and tutoring services.
    \item \textbf{User Profile Interface}: For managing personal information and preferences.
\end{itemize}

All interfaces must be responsive and accessible on desktop and mobile devices.

\subsubsection{Hardware Interfaces}
The system does not require specific hardware interfaces beyond standard computing devices with internet connectivity:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Desktop computers
    \item Laptop computers
    \item Tablets
    \item Smartphones
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Software Interfaces}
The system will interface with the following software systems:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Email Service API}: For user verification and notifications
    \item \textbf{Database Management System}: For storing user data, notes, calendar events, and forum content
    \item \textbf{File Storage System}: For storing uploaded study materials
    \item \textbf{Authentication System}: For user login and security
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Communication Interfaces}
The system will utilize the following communication protocols:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{HTTPS}: For secure data transmission between client and server
    \item \textbf{WebSocket}: For real-time notifications and messaging within study groups
    \item \textbf{SMTP}: For sending emails to users
\end{itemize}
